Privacy Policy

Radhe Discord Music Bot
Effective date: 17 October 2026

Important: This policy must match the bot’s actual code, database, logs, analytics, and hosting-provider practices. Before publishing, replace [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] and remove or revise any statement that is not accurate.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Radhe Discord Music Bot (“the Bot”) may process information when used on Discord. It does not cover Discord or third-party services, which have their own privacy policies.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on which features are enabled and how the Bot is implemented, it may process:

  • Discord server (guild) IDs and channel IDs needed to respond in the correct server or channel.
  • Discord user IDs and command details needed to execute commands, apply permissions, or troubleshoot abuse.
  • Music search terms, track titles, queue entries, and playback settings needed to provide music features.
  • Configuration values that server administrators choose to save, such as a DJ role or music channel.
  • Technical logs, error reports, and basic hosting logs generated while operating the Bot.

Do not assume that every item above is collected. The operator must verify the Bot’s actual behavior and update this list accordingly. The Bot should not request passwords or Discord account login credentials.

3. How information is used

Information may be used only as needed to operate music commands and server settings, maintain queues or preferences, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, protect service security, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Storage and retention

Some information may be processed temporarily in memory. If the Bot stores settings, logs, or other information in a database or files, the operator should keep it only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, then delete or anonymise it where appropriate. Exact storage locations and retention periods depend on the deployed code and hosting provider; verify and specify them before publishing.

5. Sharing and third parties

Information may be processed by Discord, the Bot’s hosting provider, and third-party music or media services when required for the Bot to work. The operator should not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the security and rights of users or the service. Review the terms and privacy policies of any third-party services the Bot actually uses.

6. Security

The operator should take reasonable steps to protect stored information and bot credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Never publish bot tokens, API keys, passwords, or database credentials in public repositories or logs.

7. Your choices and requests

You can stop using the Bot by removing it from your server. For questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information the Bot operator controls, contact [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] and include enough detail to identify the relevant server or request without sending passwords or sensitive credentials. The operator will respond as required by applicable law. Some records may need to be retained where legally required or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Children and age requirements

The Bot is intended for people who are permitted to use Discord and the relevant services under the age rules and laws that apply to them. The operator does not knowingly seek to collect information from anyone who is not permitted to use the service.

9. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated as the Bot’s features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
